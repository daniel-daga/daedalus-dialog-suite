# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and keep
discussion about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not
tolerated in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/daniel-daga/daedalus-dialog-suite/security/advisories/new)
(start the title with "Conduct:"), or with GitHub's "Report content" on the
comment in question. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this
code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a contributor, following
the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
